# Making BenTasker.co.uk Available As A Gemini Capsule

www.bentasker.co.uk has long been available via Tor and as an I2P Eepsite.
Although access via those networks requires appropriate software, the only real change is the transport layer: the site still ultimately talks HTTP and serves HTML to the user's browser. The site even looks the same.
This time, though, is different: my posts are now available as a Gemini Capsule (gemini://gem.bentasker.co.uk), serving gemtext over Gemini Protocol.
One of the things that I noticed while browsing around Gemspace is how unintrusive the browsing experience is: 

no javascript 
no popups or modals
no autoplaying videos
there isn't even site specific UI design (gemtext is rendered however...
