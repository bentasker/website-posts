# Telling A Happier Solar Battery Story

I've been a bit down on our choice to install a solar battery and my most recent attempt to make the finances work didn't really make things any better.
Ultimately, though, that money is gone and financial savings are not the only benefit that a battery can bring, so I decided to try and focus more on other aspects instead.
One of the main benefits of a solar install, in general, is that it offsets carbon: allowing us to consume low-carbon electricity rather than energy freshly zapped out of a gas turbine. The battery plays a role in that capability too.
