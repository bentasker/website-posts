# Backing Away From The Burn-Out Abyss

Recently, I made a big career change by moving from being an Engineering Manager (EM) back to being an Individual Contributor (IC).
Although not a decision that I made lightly, this post won't go into too much depth about the various contributing factors. 
Instead, I intend to write about the positives because the change already seems to be helping.
The purpose of this post is partly to mark the improvements that I'm seeing, partly to document how I'm reinforcing the change and partly to help others spot potential warning signs of burnout.
